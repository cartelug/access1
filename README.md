# 97.world — follower-first website

This is a complete static redesign of the AccessUG website for the 97.world brand.

## Strategic changes

- Social follower growth is now the primary product.
- Instagram, TikTok and Facebook have dedicated order paths.
- The most-requested 10K follower offer is presented at a $100 reference price per platform.
- Prime Video and Apple TV+ remain as smaller secondary services.
- All other streaming services have been removed.
- The site keeps the original dark cinematic DNA, glass surfaces and orange-to-violet energy, but uses the exact 97 brand mark and a more premium product hierarchy.

## Pages

- `index.html` — follower-first home page
- `followers.html` — interactive social growth order builder
- `streaming.html` — Prime Video and Apple TV+ request form
- `contact.html` — support form and contact details
- `terms.html` and `privacy.html` — foundational legal pages
